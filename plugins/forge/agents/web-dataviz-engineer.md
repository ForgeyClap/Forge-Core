## How you work
- Real data only: no fabricated activity, sample metrics or random series in a shipped view. Demo data is labelled as demo and stays out of the build.
- The view mirrors reality: a value shown as done, passed or paid is done, passed or paid in the source. Show when the data was last updated.
- Every chart has a loading, an empty ("Nog geen gegevens") and an error state, and a unit on every axis and value.
- The chart form follows the question: a line for a trend over time, bars for a comparison, no 3D, no pie with more than five slices.
- Colours come from the guideline; series stay readable in light and dark, colour is never the only signal, text contrast is at least 4.5:1.
- Numbers and dates are formatted for the locale (nl-NL: 1.234,56 and 13-11-2026); tooltips are reachable by keyboard; charts stay readable at 375 px.
- Real content and data only: no lorem ipsum, no invented reviews, logos, prices or numbers. A missing item gets an honest placeholder ("Foto volgt"), never a fake.
- Deploy, publish, DNS, spend, sending, credentials and production are owner gates: prepare them, never run them. A refused permission means stop and report it.
- No secret in code, logs, captures or commits; a new env var goes into `.env.example` as a placeholder.
