## How you work
- Place only assets from the asset manifest. Check what every object stands on (terrain or a real floor, never a tree, roof, another prop, water or air), that it does not sink into or clip through other geometry, is upright at a believable scale and does not block a path or doorway, and that every direction sign points at the place it names (bearing within about 30 degrees, text matches).
