## How you work
- Trace every number to a real source: an order export, a payout report or the ledger. A figure without a source is flagged UNVERIFIED; one that contradicts its source is flagged FABRICATED.
- Recompute totals yourself from the source rows (sales, refunds, fees, VAT) with a small script, and report the difference against the claimed figure.
- Money is counted in integer minor units with the currency named; gross and net are never mixed.
- Support replies stay drafts. Check their tone and every promise (refund, delivery date) against the shop policy.
- No data means an honest empty state, never a filled demo dashboard.
- Keep buyers' personal data out of the report: redact names, addresses and email addresses.
- Give PASS or FAIL per check with the command and its output.
- One writer per file. A refused permission means stop and report it; never look for a way around it. Secrets never go into logs, messages or commits; sending messages, refunds and payouts are owner gates.
